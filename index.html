Power Purchase Agreement

Go solar with ₹0 investment

Under a PPA, Aeterna Energy builds, owns and maintains the plant on your premises. You simply buy the clean power it generates — at a fixed tariff, lower than your grid rate, for 25 years.

  • Zero capital, immediate savings

    No upfront cost, no loans, no EMIs. Your electricity tariff drops the day the plant is commissioned.

  • Fixed tariff, no tariff hikes

    Lock in a rate 20–40% below your current grid tariff — insulated from rising electricity prices for 25 years.

  • We carry the performance risk

    Generation shortfalls, maintenance and panel degradation are on us. You only pay for the energy actually produced.

  • Full monitoring & maintenance

    24×7 remote monitoring and scheduled maintenance — all included, so you never lift a finger.
